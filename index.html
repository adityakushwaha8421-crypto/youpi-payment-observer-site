STEP TWO

Connect the phone

✧

Three steps. Then alerts arrive on their own.

  1. 01

    Run the gateway

    On your computer: npm run dev. The workspace at localhost:5002 shows phone status and alerts.

    ON YOUR COMPUTER
  2. 02

    Pair and allow access

    Open the app, scan the pairing QR from the merchant dashboard, then tap Enable Paytm Merchant notifications. SMS permission is optional.

    ON THE PHONE
  3. 03

    Receive a payment

    Paytm for Business shows the alert. The app forwards payer, amount and time to your gateway.

    WAITING
⌁

An alert is not a settlement.Alerts without a bank reference are marked “Needs bank reference”. Bank SMS or statement reconciliation still confirms money.
